Privacy, terms and credits

Privacy policy

This website is a prototype. Your shortlist, viewings and enquiries are kept only in this browser tab (sessionStorage). They are never sent anywhere, and they disappear when you close the tab or choose Reset demo data in the footer.

When the site goes live, Lava will use the name, email and phone number you send only to deliver the brochure, confirm viewings and contact you about the project. Your details will never be sold or shared with third parties other than the official sales agent. You will be able to ask to see, correct or delete them at any time by calling 0901 234 567.
